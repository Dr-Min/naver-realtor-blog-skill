# Official Sources

Last verified: 2026-08-18.

The workflow treats Naver Blog-specific help pages as primary for Blog search behavior. Naver Search Advisor covers broader web-content quality and spam guidance and is used as a supporting source, not as a claim that Blog ranking uses an identical rule set.

## Naver Blog search

- [C-Rank introduction](https://help.naver.com/service/5626/contents/22927?lang=ko)
- [D.I.A. introduction](https://help.naver.com/service/5626/contents/22926?osType=COMMONOS)
- [Blog search restriction criteria](https://help.naver.com/service/5626/contents/22928?lang=ko&osType=COMMONOS)

The D.I.A. page describes factors including topical fit, experience information, information completeness, document intent, relative abuse signals, originality, and timeliness. The restriction page states that fixed top exposure does not exist, all posts are not guaranteed exposure, and excessive keywords, hidden keywords, machine-generated mass content, and non-experience manuscripts may be restricted.

## Naver Search Advisor

- [Content creation recommendations](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/content-basic)
- [Web-content spam examples](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/content-abusing)
- [Content markup](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/markup-content)

The content recommendations emphasize expertise and experience, topical consistency, transparency, readable structure, current information, and meaningful human contribution when AI tools are used. They also warn that unrelated popular terms and repeated words do not guarantee ranking and that important information should appear as text rather than only in images.

## Interpretation policy

This repository does not derive a hidden ranking formula from these sources. It uses them to support conservative operational controls:

- no top-exposure promise;
- no keyword stuffing;
- no hidden keywords;
- no mass regional-template substitution;
- no fabricated experience;
- no copied or mechanically rewritten source content;
- explicit disclosure for fictional demonstrations;
- human facts and perspective required before publication;
- current prices and availability must be rechecked.

Links and wording may change. Re-verify official pages when changing a rule or publishing a new release.
